About Trimbakeshwar Jyotirlinga

Trimbakeshwar Temple, located 28 km from Nashik in Maharashtra, is one of the 12 sacred Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva. What makes it unique among all Jyotirlingas is the three-faced Linga representing the divine Hindu Trinity — Lord Brahma (Creator), Lord Vishnu (Preserver), and Lord Rudra (Destroyer).

The temple is built in the majestic Hemadpanthi style from black basalt stone, commissioned by Peshwa Nana Saheb between 1755-1786. It sits at the foothills of the Brahmagiri Hills, from where the sacred River Godavari (Dakshin Ganga) originates.

The Kushavarta Kund, located near the temple, is considered one of the holiest bathing spots. Due to the simultaneous presence of Brahma, Vishnu, Shiva, and the sacred Godavari, Trimbakeshwar is recognized by Hindu scriptures as the only consecrated center for high-potency karmic rituals like Narayan Nagbali.
