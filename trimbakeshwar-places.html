Sacred Places of Nashik

Places in Trimbakeshwar

Explore the temples, kunds, hills, and spiritual landmarks that make Trimbakeshwar a sacred pilgrimage centre.

Trimbakeshwar Jyotirlinga Temple

One of Lord Shiva's twelve Jyotirlingas, this historic temple is revered for its unique three-faced linga representing Brahma, Vishnu, and Shiva. It is the principal sacred centre of the town.

Kushavart Kund

Kushavart Kund is a sacred water tank associated with the Godavari and an important place for ritual bathing and sankalp before many ceremonies performed in Trimbakeshwar.

Brahmagiri Parvat

The Brahmagiri hills rise above Trimbakeshwar and are traditionally connected with the origin of the Godavari. The mountain is a cherished destination for pilgrims, walkers, and seekers of quiet devotion.

Gangadwar

Gangadwar, meaning the gateway of Ganga, is a revered part of the Brahmagiri pilgrimage route. Devotees visit this sacred setting to remember the divine descent and source of the Godavari.

Nilparvat

Nilparvat is a peaceful and spiritually significant hill area around Trimbakeshwar, visited by devotees seeking a contemplative connection with the sacred landscape and Lord Shiva.

Nivruttinath Maharaj Mandir

This temple honours Sant Nivruttinath Maharaj, a revered saint of the Nath tradition and elder brother and spiritual guide of Sant Dnyaneshwar. It is an important place of bhakti and remembrance.

Gajanan Maharaj Mandir

The Gajanan Maharaj Mandir is a devotional place where visitors come for darshan, prayer, and quiet reflection in the spiritual atmosphere of Trimbakeshwar.
