Poojas Performed with Devotion

Akolkar Guruji offer traditional Vedic poojas, yajnas, yagas, and samskaras for devotees visiting from across India and around the world. Contact us to discuss your pooja requirements.

Trusted by Devotees Worldwide

Devotees from Every Walk of Life

Devotees from India and around the world visit Akolkar Guruji for pooja at Trimbakeshwar. Esteemed visitors have included Balasaheb Thackeray, Devendra Fadnavis, Chhagan Bhujbal, Ekta Kapoor, and many more.
