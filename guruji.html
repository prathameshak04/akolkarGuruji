A Life Dedicated to Vedic Tradition

About Guruji

वे. मू. लोकेश शामराज अकोलकर is a Vedic scholar, purohit, teacher, and guide serving devotees from Trimbakeshwar.

ॐ

वे. मू. लोकेश शामराज अकोलकर

Born 30 April 1966, Guruji is based at 157, Vedvastu, Laxmi Narayan Chowk, Main Road, Trimbakeshwar, Nashik - 422212.

A Vedic Foundation

His learning is rooted in the Shukla Yajurveda, Madhyandina Shakha, with study of Ghana, Shatapatha Brahmana, Karmakanda, and related Vedic traditions.

Education & Qualifications

  • M.A. in Shukla Yajurveda Shastri from Sampurnanand Sanskrit University, Varanasi.
  • Diploma in Temple Management from Somnath Sanskrit University, Somnath, Gujarat.

Guided by Great Teachers

  • Vedacharya Gajanan Shastri Godshe, Varanasi.
  • Vaidik Samrat Shri Krishna Shastri Godshe, Nashik.

Service & Responsibility

  • Member of the Purohit Sangh, Trimbakeshwar.
  • Trustee of Shri Ganga-Godavari Mandir Sanstha, Trimbakeshwar.
  • Trustee of Vaidik Gyan Vigyan Sanskrit Mahavidyalaya, Nashik.

Examining & Teaching

  • Examiner for Vedashastrottejak Sabha, Pune.
  • Examiner for Kalidas Sanskrit University, Nagpur.

Vedic Service Across Many Places

Guruji has performed Veda and Ghana-Jata recitations in Pune, Trimbakeshwar, Varanasi, Dombivli, Pandharpur, and many other places. He also serves as an acharya for yajnas, yagas, and samskaras across India and abroad.
