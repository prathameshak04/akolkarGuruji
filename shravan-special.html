Why Shravan Is Auspicious

Shravan is considered one of the most sacred months dedicated to Lord Shiva. Devotees observe prayers, fasting, mantra chanting, and Abhishek to express devotion and seek divine grace.

Mondays in Shravan, known as Shravan Somvar, are especially revered. Rudra Abhishek during this month is traditionally performed for inner peace, health, strength, prosperity, and the removal of obstacles.

Why Abhishek Matters

Abhishek is a sacred offering in which the Shiva Linga is bathed with pure substances while Vedic mantras are recited. It is an act of surrender, gratitude, prayer, and spiritual purification.
